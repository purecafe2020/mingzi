Why this name works

The surname 雷 (léi) is one of the classic Chinese surnames and starts with the same sound as Lily. 蕊 (ruǐ) carries the sense of flower bud; quiet promise, and 悦 (yuè) carries delight — together the given name reads like a small blessing, not a transliteration. Both characters are common in real given names, so nothing marks the name as foreign.
